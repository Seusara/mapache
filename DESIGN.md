## Brand & Style
This design system embodies a warm editorial, tactile, and literary experience built as a digital love letter. It merges the deliberate craftsmanship of botanical field notebooks with intimate developer sensibilities. The visual world rejects generic romantic tropes—eschewing bright reds, hot pinks, and cartoon hearts—in favor of golden-hour florals, sunlit meadow foliage, and the gentle charm of a woodland raccoon companion.

The audience is singular and deeply personal: an intimate recipient who cherishes thoughtful prose, quiet beauty, and nuanced craft. The experience should evoke warmth, safety, wonder, and the delicate rustle of handmade cotton rag paper resting beneath evening sunlight.

The design movement is **Warm Editorial & Tactile Paper**:
- **Tactile warmth:** Subtle fibrous textures, generous literary margins, and layered cream tones simulate the quiet tactile luxury of fine archival stationery.
- **Botanical delicacy:** Organic curves, whisper-thin floral dividers, and gentle golden highlights mirror petals filtering the last light of sunset.
- **Code intimacy:** Monospaced typographic annotations resemble private developer marginalia—inline variables, timestamps, and commit-like dedications that frame high-literature prose with technological tenderness.

## Colors
The palette captures a secret garden bathed in golden hour, anchored by soft stone neutrals inspired by raccoon fur.

### Tone Roles & Intent
- **Background & Canvas:**
  - Base Canvas (`#FAF7F0`): Warm archival cream reminiscent of untreated cotton paper.
  - Layered Surface (`#F5EEDF`): Deeper parchment tone for recessed panels, envelope pockets, and structural dividers.
  - Surface Card (`#FFFDF8`): Luminous sunlit paper surface that rises above the ground plane.
- **Golden Flora (Primary System):**
  - Primary Accent (`#F59E0B`): Radiant marigold/yellow flower hue for focal CTAs, drop caps, and active states.
  - Soft Bloom (`#FCD34D`): Petal wash for delicate button backgrounds, subtle badge fills, and hover tints.
  - Golden Highlight (`#FDE68A`): Shimmer accents, keyline borders, and text selection glows.
- **Garden Foliage (Secondary System):**
  - Fresh Leaf (`#65A30D`): Organic sap green for living status indicators, sprig accents, and success cues.
  - Mint Whisper (`#A7F3D0`): Delicate botanical wash for inline tags, micro-badges, and calming backdrop glows.
  - Deep Forest (`#166534`): Grounded emerald for high-contrast botanical headers and dark foliage accents.
- **Raccoon & Ink (Tertiary & Neutral System):**
  - Charcoal Ink (`#292524`): Dominant text color; softer than harsh pitch-black, carrying the warmth of fountain pen ink on parchment.
  - Soft Fur Neutral (`#78716C`): Midtone slate-taupe for secondary text, author captions, and inactive elements.
  - Whispering Fur (`#D6D3D1`): Pale neutral for subtle hair-thin borders, code comment blocks, and delicate shadows.

## Typography
The typographic triad balances poetic passion, effortless readability, and programmer craft:

1. **Playfair Display (Editorial Headlines):** Used for chapter openers, heartfelt epistles, and expressive titles. Set predominantly in regular and medium weights to prevent heavy commercial aggressiveness. Italicized cuts are reserved for intimate phrases and poetic emphasis.
2. **Manrope (Narrative Body):** Provides geometric yet humanist clarity. Its generous x-height and open apertures guarantee long-form legibility across memoirs, letters, and timelines without distracting from the editorial warmth.
3. **JetBrains Mono (Developer Marginalia):** Injected for timestamps, location coordinates, git-inspired commit hashes (`commit: to-my-favorite-person`), inline variable metaphors (`const heart = infinite;`), and status tags.

### Micro-Typographic Rules
- Paragraph blocks must maintain a maximum line measure of 62 characters for optimal reading rhythm.
- Display numbers and chapter numerals should always be set in `Playfair Display` italic with delicate warm tracking.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout reflects the pacing of an artisanal literary publication: intentional pauses, asymmetric vignettes, and unhurried whitespace.

### Grid & Flow
- **System:** 12-column adaptive grid on desktop with generous page margins (`margin: 2rem` scaling to `4rem` on wide displays) to preserve an airy, quiet ambiance.
- **Mobile Reflow:** Single column editorial flow with edge margins of `1.25rem` (`20px`). Content wraps into full-width letter tiles and scrollable memory cards.
- **Reading Measure:** Text-heavy sections are constrained to a centered 680px container regardless of viewport size, creating the visual silhouette of an open book.

### Vertical Rhythm
- Spacing between disparate story chapters utilizes `space-xl` multiplied dynamically (up to 6rem on desktop) to allow emotional decompression between memories.
- Micro-elements (such as tags, author stamps, and botanical glyphs) conform strictly to 4px multiples via `space-xs` through `space-md`.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is rendered through physical paper layers, light infiltration, and diffuse golden-hour shadows rather than stark digital drops.

### Architectural Levels
- **Base Level (Canvas):** Tone `#FAF7F0` with faint, organic noise simulation mimicking handmade stationery.
- **Sunlit Parchment (Cards & Envelopes):** Tone `#FFFDF8` elevated via warm, dual-phase ambient shadows tinted with umber:
  `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(41, 37, 36, 0.04), 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.08);`
- **Floating Overlays & Floating Petals:** Soft blurs (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) across translucent `#FFFDF8`/85 surfaces, encircled by 1px ghost borders in `#FDE68A`/40.
- **Borders & Rules:** Replaces heavy borders with ultra-fine hairline dividers (`1px solid #D6D3D1` with 60% opacity) or trailing dotted lines in `#78716C` at 30% opacity.

## Shapes
A roundedness factor of `2` (8px / 0.5rem default, scaling to 16px / 1rem for `rounded-lg` and 24px / 1.5rem for `rounded-xl`) produces an organic, hand-trimmed contour reminiscent of pressed botanical paper and smoothed river stones.

### Signature Contours
- **Keepsake Cards:** Border radius `rounded-lg` (16px) with intentional soft corner clipping.
- **Botanical Badges & Chips:** Full pill contour (`9999px`) to echo the soft silhouette of fallen flower petals.
- **Code Badges & Snippet Blocks:** Gentle `rounded-sm` (6px) corners to preserve their structural, programmatic identity without feeling jarringly sharp.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary ("Pressed Bloom"):** Background `#F59E0B` graduating into subtle `#FCD34D`, text `#292524`, font `Manrope` medium (14px). Soft pill shape with an ambient warm amber glow on hover. Click state compresses gently by 2% (`scale(0.98)`).
- **Secondary ("Botanical Outline"):** Background transparent, 1px border in `#65A30D` (40%), text `#166534`. Hover transitions to `#F5EEDF` fill with green stroke brightening to 100%.
- **Tertiary ("Compiler Link"):** `JetBrains Mono` 12px, charcoal ink `#292524`, prefaced with an inline `//` or `>` symbol. Underline is an offset golden hairline that expands from center on hover.

### Chips & Tags
- **Foliage Chip:** Background `#A7F3D0` at 35% opacity, text `#166534`, font `JetBrains Mono` 11px uppercase, border radius 9999px. Used for dates, seasons, and mood markers.
- **Raccoon Footprint Tag:** Background `#FAF7F0`, border 1px solid `#D6D3D1`, text `#78716C`. Accompanied by a tiny paw or botanical SVG icon.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Fountain Pen Inputs:** Pristine `#FFFDF8` background, 1px hairline border in `#D6D3D1`. Focus state softly transitions the border to `#F59E0B` alongside a 3px diffused outer halo of `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.15)`. Floating placeholder renders in `Playfair Display` italic.
- **Selection Checks:** Custom leaf-shaped checkbox. Unchecked: `#D6D3D1` fine stroke. Checked: fills with `#65A30D` containing an ivory checkmark.

### Cards & Narrative Letters
- **Letter Sheet:** Layered `#FFFDF8` card with 24px inner padding, bounded by a 1px border in `#FDE68A` (50%). Features a top-right developer metadata watermark in `JetBrains Mono` (e.g., `loc: 40.7128° N, temp: 21°C, mood: beloved`).
- **Interactive Memory Polaroid:** Matte card frame with a slightly thicker bottom border, subtle paper drop-shadow, and a photo slot framed with a 1px border. Rotates slightly (between -1deg and +1.5deg) upon hover.

### Domain-Specific Components
- **Floating Petal Stream:** Subtle canvas overlay rendering slow, physics-gentle falling and drifting yellow blossoms that react minimally to cursor velocity.
- **Console of Devotion:** A collapsible terminal drawer framed in `#292524` with text in `#FDE68A` and `#A7F3D0`, exposing sweet system logs (e.g., `[runtime] Mapachita detected. Allocating maximum love...`).
- **Wax Seal Audio / Toggle:** A circular 40px interactive stamp in warm golden amber (`#F59E0B`) with an embossed raccoon silhouette, used to trigger ambient sounds or play curated voice notes.
